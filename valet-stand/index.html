Valet stand in solid wood

A homemade take on the Pinetti Němý sluha: a mahogany frame with a carved tray, a low base and wooden hangers. Seven rounds of renders explore woods, tray shapes, hanger designs and small joinery details — each image shows how the piece would actually be built.

The brief

  • 01Mahogany frame; nothing darker than the mahogany
  • 02Solid wood only — never veneer
  • 03No metal except the hanger hooks
  • 04At most three woods per version; toned down, not flashy

How these were made

Modelled in Autodesk Fusion (proportions taken from the original), rendered in Blender Cycles in a white-ash room. Trays, segments, laminations and inlays are real geometry with grain direction and end grain, not painted textures.

Woods & prices

Wood textures come from photos of actual boards; costs are rough timber estimates at median Kč/m³ from the exotic-woods price gallery, metal and finish excluded.

Reference

Inspired by the Pinetti valet stand in walnut, leather and brass (perdonahome.cz). Built with Claude Code.
