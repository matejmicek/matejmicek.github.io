 THE SMALL PRINT, MADE USEFUL

Know what’s confirmed.

Confirmed

An organiser or official registration page publishes the date. A festival date can span several days; check the start of your chosen distance.

Calendar listing

A race calendar publishes a date that has not been corroborated on the organiser’s site. Treat it as provisional.

Date TBA

An event to watch for 2027 whose exact date has not been found. Some editions are announced; others are recurring possibilities. Expected months and reference courses come from prior editions where noted. These entries never appear on a specific calendar day.

Scope

Events starting 22 September 2026 through 31 December 2027. Trail, mountain, ultra, backyard, timed events and selected mixed-surface relays. Broad Czech coverage and a selection of worthwhile cross-border trips; not a complete registry of every local race. Hiking events are labelled in the notes. Events already underway before the window are excluded.

Routes & teams

Direct GPX files are distinguished from organiser route pages. Previous-edition tracks are for reconnaissance only. Team sizes, course lengths and elevation are shown as published, with gaps left explicit. Festival totals and relay totals are not individual running distances.

Driving

Approximate road distances and driving times from central Prague, estimated for planning and rounded. Not live navigation. Open the directions link for your actual route. Allow for traffic, border delays, parking and race shuttles.

Shortlist & exports

Your shortlist stays in this browser. Calendar exports contain dated entries only; provisional dates are marked tentative. Research is a snapshot from 22 September 2026, not automatically refreshed.
