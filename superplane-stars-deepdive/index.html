03/Bought-Star Forensics

193 stars look bought. Plus 8 are too thin to read.

A separate read of the suspect set, looking for paid-star vendor signatures — the kind of stars you can buy on Fiverr / GitStars / starseller services. Five lenses: account age, profile completeness, contribution graph, creation cohorts, and shared starred-repo fingerprints across suspect accounts.

Smoking gun: vendor portfolio fingerprint. Of 193 paid-star candidates, 24 also star MemoriLabs/Memori and 22 also star daytonaio/daytona — both unrelated AI/devtools repos. That is the textbook signature of a paid-star vendor selling bundled "AI startup" star packages: the same shell accounts get pointed at successive paying customers. Independent and concerning. 14/AI in the Codebase

Cursor and Copilot are not assistants here — they're committers.

SuperPlane has integrated AI coding agents directly into the contribution flow. Both cursor[bot] and Copilot open PRs against main, with merged ones counted in the contributor list.
