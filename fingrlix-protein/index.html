kcal / g B

A browser extension for fingrlix.com. It puts calories per gram of protein next to every meal on the menu, then folds the whole menu into a scatter plot on one click.

Lower is leaner. Protein is B, for bílkoviny.

What it does

A menu tells you calories. Calories on their own say nothing about the thing you are actually buying a meal for. The extension divides one by the other and shows the result as a coloured badge on every card, live on the page: ≤ 13 ≤ 18 > 18 kcal per gram of protein.

Clicking the GRAF pill morphs the menu in place. Every food tile slides out of the grid to its own position on protein against calories and shrinks into a chip, with two benchmark lines drawn through the origin. Anything below a line beats that benchmark on protein per calorie.

The benchmarks

Reference kcal g protein kcal / g
🍗 Roast chicken breast, rice and broccoli, 150 g each 496 54.2 9.2
🥛 Vilgain Protein Milkshake vanilla, 330 ml 261 33 7.9

Chicken, rice and broccoli from USDA per-100 g values. The Vilgain figures are the bottle's own nutrition label.

The finding

Every number in the video is real, read off the live menu for Monday 21 September. Out of the sixteen items on that day, exactly one sits below both benchmark lines, and it is the plain grilled chicken side at 6.6. The best full meal is the Caesar salad at 12.1. Breakfast cake comes in at 25.2.

How it is built

Manifest V3 content script, no dependencies and no build step. It reads the macro line straight out of the DOM, survives the site's re-renders through a MutationObserver, and the chart is plain SVG with the chips laid out by a small overlap-relaxation pass so nothing sits on top of anything else.
